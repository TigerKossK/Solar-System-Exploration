I want to create a new cinematic section for the Solar System website using Higgsfield for the video generation.

Before we generate the final video, I want to first create several still-image variants of the scene. We will review and discuss those images together, choose the best visual direction, and only then move on to generating the video.

The video will become the second major section of the website, directly after the first main page/opening section.

Overall website flow

The experience should work like this:

First main page → scroll → cinematic transition → Solar System video → scroll → video progresses → Earth becomes the focus → Earth continues orbiting → only then reveal the main menu and remaining website content.

The important part is that the video itself should be controlled by the user's scroll position.

This should NOT simply be a normal autoplay video.

1. The video concept

The video should be a cinematic journey through our Solar System.

At the beginning, the camera should be positioned in a wide orbit-view of the entire Solar System, where the Sun and all planets can be seen.

The camera should then begin moving toward the inner Solar System and eventually focus on Earth.

The camera movement should feel:

very dynamic
cinematic
fast but controlled
highly focused
smooth
immersive
physically believable
visually impressive

The transition should feel like the camera is actually travelling through space rather than simply zooming into a static image.

The Solar System shown in the video must match the visual identity of our existing Solar System website.

All planets should have the same general visual/design language as the existing Solar System project.

Do not create a completely different visual style just because the video is generated externally.

The video should feel like it is a natural extension of our existing Solar System website.

The entire video must follow the existing color palette of the website.

4. Camera journey

The camera sequence should roughly follow this progression:

Stage 1 — Solar System overview

Start with a wide shot of the entire Solar System.

The Sun should be visible and all planets should be recognizable in their orbital positions.

The camera should establish the environment before beginning its journey.

Stage 2 — Dynamic approach

The camera starts travelling through the Solar System.

It should move toward Earth with a strong sense of speed and direction.

The camera should pass through the planetary system rather than simply scaling the entire scene.

The movement should feel intentional, with Earth gradually becoming the main target.

Stage 3 — Earth approach

The camera gets closer and closer to Earth.

Earth should become increasingly detailed as the camera approaches.

The camera should remain strongly focused on Earth throughout this part.

Stage 4 — Earth arrival

The camera eventually reaches Earth and transitions into a close orbital view.

At this point, the aggressive camera movement should settle down.

Stage 5 — Earth orbit

The camera is now focused on Earth.

Earth continues rotating/orbiting naturally.

The scene should become calmer compared with the previous dynamic journey.

This is where the user should feel that they have finally "arrived" at Earth.

5. Scroll-trigger behavior

This is extremely important.

The entire cinematic sequence should be controlled by scroll position.

For example:

At the beginning of the section → Solar System overview
User scrolls → camera starts moving
More scrolling → camera travels toward Earth
More scrolling → camera reaches Earth
More scrolling → Earth becomes the primary focus
Further scrolling → Earth continues its orbital scene
After the cinematic sequence finishes → reveal the main menu/content

The user should effectively control the camera journey through scrolling.

The animation should feel scrubbed by the scroll, rather than playing independently.

If the user scrolls backward, the animation should also be able to move backward smoothly.

6. Main menu reveal

The main menu and the rest of the website should NOT appear immediately after the first page.

The order must be:

First page → Solar System cinematic section → Earth focus/orbit → main menu and remaining content.

The main menu should only become visible after the cinematic sequence has reached its intended endpoint.

The reveal should also be smooth and cinematic rather than suddenly appearing.

If the video needs optimization, compression, different resolutions, poster frames, or another technique, determine that before implementation.
