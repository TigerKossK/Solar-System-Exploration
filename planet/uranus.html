Uranus

Ice giant · The tilted ice giant · William Herschel, 1781 — the first planet found with a telescope

An icy world of water, methane and ammonia, Uranus is tipped almost completely on its side, so it appears to roll along its orbit.

Atmosphere & magnetosphere

Atmosphere

About 83% hydrogen, 15% helium and 2% methane. Methane absorbs red light, giving Uranus its pale cyan colour.

Magnetic field

A lopsided field, tilted 59° from the axis and offset from the planet's centre.

Rings

Make-up

Composition

Water, methane and ammonia ices over a small rocky core; methane tints it blue-green.

Notable features

  • Miranda — a moon of chasms and cliffs more than 10 km high.

Fun facts

  • Uranus's extreme tilt gives each pole roughly 42 years of continuous sunlight, then 42 of darkness.
  • It rotates almost on its side, as if rolling along its orbit.
  • Uranus was the first planet discovered with a telescope, by William Herschel in 1781.
  • Methane in its air absorbs red light, giving the planet its cyan colour.
  • Its magnetic field is tilted 59° from its axis and offset from the planet's centre.
