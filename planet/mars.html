Mars

Terrestrial planet · The rusted frontier · Known since antiquity

A cold desert world half the size of Earth, Mars carries dry riverbeds and the largest volcano in the Solar System — evidence of a more active past.

Atmosphere & magnetosphere

Atmosphere

95% carbon dioxide, 2.7% nitrogen, 1.6% argon. Far too thin to hold liquid water on the surface today.

Surface pressure ≈ 0.6% of Earth's Relative to Earth's 1 bar

Magnetic field

No global field today — only patchy magnetism frozen into the crust.

Make-up

Composition

Thin CO₂ atmosphere; iron-oxide dust gives the surface its red colour.

Notable features

  • Olympus Mons — the tallest volcano in the Solar System, about 22 km high.
  • Valles Marineris — a canyon system more than 4,000 km long.

Fun facts

  • Olympus Mons on Mars is about three times the height of Mount Everest.
  • Valles Marineris is so long it would stretch across the United States.
  • Mars has seasons like Earth because its axis is tilted about 25°.
  • Its two tiny moons, Phobos and Deimos, were discovered only in 1877.
  • Phobos orbits so close that it will break apart or crash into Mars within ~50 million years.
