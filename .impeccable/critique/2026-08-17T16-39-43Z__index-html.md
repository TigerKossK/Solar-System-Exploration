## Design Specificity Verdict

**Split: an exceptionally authored core wearing an interchangeable overture that now owns the fold.**

**LLM assessment (A):** The `#menu` orrery is deeply product-specific — real orbit rings, golden-angle placement, per-family planet CSS (craters, gas-giant banding, a real Saturn ring), a live `aria-live` readout, planets as real `<a>` links (no-JS survivable), a layered plasma Sun with granulation drift. ~9/10 specificity. But the two new front-loaded beats invert it: the `.opening` curtain (dark veil + giant faint wordmark + "Scroll to enter") is the canonical premium-landing trope, and the `.journey` scrub is a generic cinematic-scroll mechanism whose specificity lives entirely in two `.webp` files. The most interchangeable material is winning the fold; the most authored material (the map) is buried ~7 screens down.

**Deterministic scan (B):** The bundled detector is missing its parser deps on this machine, so a naïve run reports a false-clean `[]` (exit 0). B supplied the deps in an isolated scratchpad (temp junction, removed after — skill dir left byte-for-byte) and ran the enhanced scan: **15 findings on the landing, 37 on `planet/earth.html`.** Dominant, highest-signal issue: **undersized-ui-text** — functional labels at **10.88px** and units at **9.28px**, below the 11px floor (2× landing, **23×** Earth page). The rest cluster into the glow/gradient family — `dark-glow` (×3 landing, ×6 earth), `radial-halo`, `gradient-text` (×2) — plus `clipped-overflow-container`, `pulsing-dot` (×2), `nested-cards`, `flat-type-hierarchy`, `all-caps-body` (×2).

**Agreement / divergence:** A and B agree on legibility — A flagged the ~11px kicker as a size/contrast risk; B quantified it sitewide with hard numbers, worst on the inner data dashboards (23 hits), directly violating the product's own WCAG-AA commitment. They diverge on the glow/halo/gradient flags: those are largely **intentional brand** here (the committed teal-void + amber-glow world), mostly false positives *as slop* — though their volume on inner pages invites a restraint pass. The detector is structurally blind to A's biggest finding — buried map / reversed narrative — because that's a flow problem, not a CSS pattern.

**Visual overlays:** none — live-browser injection skipped (Playwright not authorized). Static detector evidence only.

## Overall Impression

The core is genuinely special: the orrery *is* the navigation, it's accessible, and the visual world is committed and premium. But the two cinematic beats we just added, however well-built, now bury that core ~7 screens deep with no visible fast path, and the flight to Earth is thrown away by a hard cut into an unrelated cosmos plus a "now approaching the system" line *after* arrival. Biggest opportunity: get the map to the visitor far sooner and make the arrival pay off instead of resetting. Second: the sitewide sub-11px functional text is a concrete, detector-confirmed legibility failure that breaks your own accessibility promise.

## What's Working

1. **The orrery delivers the thesis _and_ is accessible** — real orbit rings, golden-angle placement, per-planet character, `role="group"` + `aria-label`s + `aria-live` readout, planets-as-real-links (no-JS survivable).
2. **Reduced-motion / no-JS discipline is everywhere** — opening, journey, reveal, components all degrade to static/usable.
3. **A committed, token-driven visual world** — teal void + amber Sun, layered plasma Sun with granulation drift, liquid-glass panels with true offset shadows, the `@property --size` typed orrery transition.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] The interactive map is ~7 screens deep with no visible fast path.**
- *Why:* `.opening` (180vh) + `.journey` (320vh) + `#intro` + `#warp` ≈ 700vh of non-interactive cinema before the product. On mobile `.nav__links` is `display:none` and the skip-link is invisible to touch — no discoverable way to reach the map before bouncing. Detector can't see this; it's still the top issue.
- *Fix:* Collapse to one cinematic beat and shorten hard; add a persistent, visible "Enter the map" control for mouse+touch; keep topbar "Explore" clickable throughout (drop `pointer-events:none` during opening).
- *Command:* `/impeccable distill` (+ `/impeccable adapt` for the mobile path).

**[P1] Hard cut + reversed narrative at the journey → intro → warp seam.**
- *Why:* You fly all the way to Earth, then `#intro` discards Earth for a synthetic cosmos, then `#warp` says "Approaching the system" *after* arrival. The payoff is thrown away and contradicted.
- *Fix:* Land the Earth frame into the intro (carry its glow behind the intro glass, or open the orrery focused on Earth); delete `#warp`.
- *Command:* `/impeccable animate` (seam) + `/impeccable distill` (delete warp).

**[P1] Sub-11px functional text sitewide — detector-confirmed, breaks your own AA commitment.**
- *Why:* Labels at **10.88px**, units at **9.28px** — below the floor — with **23 instances on `planet/earth.html`** and 2 on the landing. PRODUCT.md commits to WCAG-AA; this is the clearest violation, on the dashboards that are the payoff.
- *Fix:* Raise functional text to ≥12–13px (units ≥11px); widen the type hierarchy (detector: flat 1.8–2.0 ratio).
- *Command:* `/impeccable typeset`.

**[P1] Mobile: nav hidden, sub-44px targets, no touch escape.**
- *Why:* ≤720px hides `.nav__links`; ~30px `.selector` pills become the primary planet nav; smallest orrery nodes fall under 44px; no visible skip-to-map on touch.
- *Fix:* Keep a mobile nav affordance (sticky "map" button); raise pills + smallest hit areas to ≥44px; expose a visible skip control on small screens.
- *Command:* `/impeccable adapt`.

**[P2] Redundant messaging flattens hierarchy.**
- *Why:* "Eight worlds. One star." (opening) ≈ "One star. Eight worlds…" (warp); `#intro` H1 is verbatim the `#menu` H2 (heard twice by screen readers).
- *Fix:* Give each surviving beat one job; dedupe H1/H2; cut warp copy; resolve the "kickers banned" comment vs the actual `#intro` kicker.
- *Command:* `/impeccable clarify`.

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer):** May read the opening veil as the entire page — only cue is a tiny dot. After Earth, `#intro` hard-cuts it and `#warp` says "Approaching the system" (backwards). Two near-identical CTAs blur intent.

**Sam (accessibility):** *Passing:* amber `:focus-visible`, skip-link, orrery `role="group"` + `aria-label`s + `aria-live`, reduced-motion. *Failing:* only fast escape is focus-only; functional text 10.88/9.28px below floor; ~11px kicker on `--text-lo #8fa6a8` borderline for 4.5:1; `.opening__title` is a `<p>` so first screen has no heading landmark.

**Casey (distracted mobile):** Highest bounce risk — hidden nav + focus-only skip = forced ~7-screen scrub; pills/nodes under 44px; two `.webp` stills gate the scrub (black mid-flight before load); three render-blocking font families; progression cues are tiny bottom-center dots.

## Minor Observations

- Glow/halo/gradient detector flags are mostly intentional brand, not slop — but audit inner pages for restraint (`dark-glow ×6` on earth.html).
- Inconsistent cue targets: opening/journey→#intro, intro→#warp, warp→#menu, skip-link/topbar→#menu. Pick one destination.
- `main.css` comments "no kickers — banned" yet `#intro` opens with a `.kicker-label`.
- `.journey__img--wide` `transform-origin:44% 48%` is hard-coupled to the specific asset — swap the image and the push-in misses.
- `.readout__hint` "Hover or tab through the planets" — no hover on touch; consider "Tap a planet."
- `earth.html` type reads "…Our home world · Our home planet" — near-duplicate data artifact.
- Detector caveat: runs degraded (false-clean `[]`) on this machine unless its parser deps are present.

## Questions to Consider

1. If "the map is the interface," what do the opening *and* journey earn that a single 100vh hero *over the live orrery* would not?
2. What if arrival were the product — land on Earth and open Earth's data, making the flight the first act of navigation instead of a preamble?
3. On mobile the nav is hidden and the map degrades to unlabeled dots, so "the map is the interface" silently becomes "the list is the interface." Which is the real product?
